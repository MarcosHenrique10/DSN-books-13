Silvermist, the water fairy, knew just what to do. “Rosetta, come grab
 my feet,” she said.

With Rosetta holding on to her ankle, Silvermist leaned out of the
 boat and touched the raging water with her fingertips.

Using all of her water-fairy magic and strength, Silvermist made a
 water bridge. The passengers held on for dear life as the boat shot down
 this new pathway of water, avoiding the waterfall. It was a wild ride!
