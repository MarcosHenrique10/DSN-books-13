The fairies kept going through the dark and rainy night.
 When they reached the house where Lizzy lived, they sneaked
 into the kitchen. Vidia told them to be very careful. Not only
 did they need to watch out for the little girl and her father,
 they also needed to watch out for a cat.

“Cat?” Iridessa gasped. “What cat?”

“That cat!” Clank and Bobble wailed.

Just then, Mr. Twitches, the cat, came in. He was wet. And
 he was angry. The fairies backed into a corner
 and Mr. Twitches pounced!
