Trying to get away from the bee, Iridessa
knocks into a branch. Her touch gives it
a growth spurt! The branch shoots out
toward Zarina, taking all the girls with it!

Zarina is surprised, but she quickly
points her sword at the girls and whistles.

The pirates swoop in and nab the fairies
with a fishing net.

Tinker Bell cries out, “Zarina, don’t
do this! Come back home!”

Zarina laughs. “I’ll never go back to
Pixie Hollow!” she vows.
