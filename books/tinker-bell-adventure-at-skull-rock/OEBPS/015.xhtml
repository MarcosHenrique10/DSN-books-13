“Just one year ago, we’d lost everything . . . and then we
found her.” James nods at Zarina. “We needed a captain.
And when we humbly asked if she could make us fly . . .”

The fairies gasp at James’s words. Zarina has become
a pirate captain! Even more shocking, she plans to make
the whole pirate ship fly!

The pirates cheer as the ship sails toward a scary cave
called Skull Rock.
