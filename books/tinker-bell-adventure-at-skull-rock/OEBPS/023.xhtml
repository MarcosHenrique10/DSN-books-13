“Our talents!” shouts Tinker Bell to her friends.

The girls work together using their new talents to stop the ship and knock
the pirates overboard.

James sprinkles golden dust on himself and tries to fly away. But Zarina
sees one speck of Blue Dust and hurls it at the pirate. The Blue Dust mixes
with the gold dust and multiplies it. James flies out of control, right into a
big wave of water. Tink sent the wave using her new water talent.

“Can’t fly when you’re wet!” cries Zarina. “Good-bye, James!”
