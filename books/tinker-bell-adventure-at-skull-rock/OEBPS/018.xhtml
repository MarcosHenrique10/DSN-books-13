The fairies hide and watch as Zarina carries the
Blue Dust up into the Pixie Dust Tree.

“As soon as she’s gone, we’ll grab the dust and
get out of here,” says Vidia.

“Maybe we should try to talk to her,” says Tink.

“That didn’t work out at the waterfall,” Vidia
says, disagreeing.

A bee starts to buzz around Iridessa. “Shoo!”
she whispers.
