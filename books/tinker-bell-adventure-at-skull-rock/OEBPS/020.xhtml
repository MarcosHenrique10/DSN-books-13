But once the tree starts making golden
pixie dust, James turns on the tiny pirate
fairy and locks her in a lantern!

“We don’t need you anymore! Our plan
worked perfectly!” James laughs. “With a
flying ship, there’s no stopping us!”

He tosses Zarina and the lantern
overboard. “Bon voyage, little captain!”

James was just using Zarina’s talent.
He was never her friend!
