At Skull Rock, the fairies spot something that looks familiar.

“It looks like . . .” starts Iridessa.

“. . . the Pixie Dust Tree back home!” finishes Rosetta.

“Zarina must have grown it,” adds Silvermist.

“So that’s how they’re going to fly: She’s going to make pixie dust!”
Tink exclaims.

Zarina will be able to create lots of golden pixie dust by pouring
some of the Blue Dust she stole into the tree.
