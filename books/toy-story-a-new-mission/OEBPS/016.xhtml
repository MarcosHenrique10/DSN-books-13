They landed on the side of the road, and as night fell, they made
 a tent out of a newspaper they found. The cadets went to sleep, but
 Sarge stayed awake wondering when—and where—they’d find their
 new home.

At last, Sarge fell asleep. But the next morning, he and his men
 awoke to a loud rumble. Moments later, their tent was lifted into the
 air and then tossed into a trash can with Sarge still inside!
