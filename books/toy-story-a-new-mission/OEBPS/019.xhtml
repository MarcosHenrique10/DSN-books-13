As soon as they could, the troops jumped off the truck and landed
 on a wall. Maybe leaving Andy’s house wasn’t such a good idea after all,
 Sarge thought. But just then they heard something that made them stop
 and turn.

The soldiers peered over the wall and gasped. They’d landed right
 next to a beautiful playground! Could this be their new home? they
 thought, as they parachuted down for a closer look.
