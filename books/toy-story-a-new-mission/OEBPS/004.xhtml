Andy was leaving for college, and his toys were heading to the attic.
 But Sarge and his Green Army Men had a different plan. They climbed
 up to the windowsill.

“What are you doing?” asked Buzz.

“We’re moving on,” Sarge replied.

Sarge explained that the platoon’s tour of duty in Andy’s room was
 over. It was time he and his troops took up a new mission.

With one last salute, they jumped off the windowsill, opened their
 parachutes and sailed across the city.
