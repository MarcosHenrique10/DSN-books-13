The boy and his mother went to a bakery where the baker gave the
 boy a giant cookie. As he reached for it, the boy knocked the bag over
 and the army men tumbled to the floor.

After the boy left, the baker spotted the toys beside the counter.

New decorations! the baker thought as he scooped them off the floor.
 Then he carried them into the kitchen, washed them off, and put them
 on a cake.
