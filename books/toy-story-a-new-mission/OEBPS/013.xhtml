Before the army men could retreat, a pizza delivery truck screeched
 to a stop just beside them. They watched as a waiter came out of the
 restaurant with a tall stack of pizza boxes in his arms.

“Take these pizzas to the Wilderness Explorer picnic,” the waiter told
 the driver. “And hurry! There are a lot of hungry kids waiting for lunch.”

Sarge looked at his men and smiled. Perfect! they all thought.
