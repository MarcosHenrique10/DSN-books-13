Sarge and his troops climbed onto the top of the pizza truck, but as
 soon as the truck hit a bump, one of the soldier’s parachutes opened.

The wind was strong and began to pull him off.

“Go on without me!” the cadet called.

“Negative,” said Sarge. “A good soldier never leaves a man behind.”
 He and the other cadet let go of the truck, held onto the soldier and
 let the wind carry them off.
