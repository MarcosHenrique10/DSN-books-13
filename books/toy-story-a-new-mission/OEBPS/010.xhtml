The baker put the cake in a box and a few hours later, Sarge and his
 men found themselves in front of a very happy birthday boy.

“Green Army Men! Cool!” the boy exclaimed.

Excited, he plucked the soldiers off the cake and set them aside.

“This could be it, men,” Sarge whispered. “This boy could lead us to
 our new home!”

The soldiers nodded in agreement. Their mission was back on track.
