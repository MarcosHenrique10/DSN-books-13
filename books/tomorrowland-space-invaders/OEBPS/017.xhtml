The Callisto family hurries to the Main Power
 Axis Room—the brain of the ship. But there’s
 something in there that doesn’t belong.

“How did something that big get onto our ship?”
 Loretta wonders.

“It must be connected to every system on the
 Stellosphere,” Phoebe says. Leo grabs one of the
 creature’s many arms, but it pushes him away and
 slams the door!
