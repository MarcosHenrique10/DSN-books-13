While visiting an unknown planet, Miles finds a cool
new rock to add to his collection. But not long after
he brings the rock home, the Stellosphere goes out
of control! Doors start slamming, the lights flicker on
and off, and something has taken over the navigation
systems. Miles’s rock is nowhere to be found. . . .
Could that rock be the
spaceship invader?
