This is what the letters said:

WHOSO PULLETH OUT THIS SWORD
 OF THIS STONE AND ANVIL IS RIGHTWISE
 KING BORN OF ALL ENGLAND.

Sir Ector read that. And so did all the other
 noblemen. Now they knew. The tournament wasn’t
 the thing whereby to choose a king. The sword was!

But how could Wart have pulled it out? There
 must be some mistake.
