The third little pig was a sober little pig. He was
 building a house, too, but he was building his of
 bricks. He did not mind hard work. He wanted a
 stout little, strong little house, for he knew that in
 the woods nearby there lived a big bad wolf who
 liked nothing better than to catch little pigs and eat
 them up!

So slap, slosh, slap! Away he worked, laying
 bricks and smoothing mortar between them.
