“Who’s there?” called the second little pig.

“I’m a poor little sheep, with no place to sleep.
 Please open the door and let me in,” said the big
 bad wolf in a sweet little voice.

The little pig peeked through a crack of the door,
 and he could see the wolf’s big black paws and large
 sharp fangs.

“Not by the hair of my chinny-chin-chin!”
