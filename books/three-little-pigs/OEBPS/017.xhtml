“Then I’ll huff, and I’ll puff, and I’ll blow your
 house in!” roared the wolf.

And he did. He blew the little straw house all
 to pieces!

Away raced the little pig to his brother’s house
 of sticks. No sooner was he in the door, when
 knock, knock, knock! There was the big bad wolf!

But of course, the little pigs would not let him
 come in.
