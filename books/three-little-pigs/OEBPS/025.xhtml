This made the big bad wolf perfectly furious!

“Now by the hair of my chinny-chin-chin,” he
 roared, “I’ll huff, and I’ll puff, and I’ll blow your
 house in!”

So the big bad wolf huffed and he PUFFED, and
 he puffed and he HUFFED, but he could not blow
 down that little house of bricks! How could he get in?
 At last he thought of the chimney!

So up he climbed, quietly. Then with a snarl,
 down he jumped—right into a kettle of boiling water!
