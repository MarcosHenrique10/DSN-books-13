“Then I’ll huff, and I’ll puff, and I’ll blow your
 house in!” cried the angry old wolf.

So he huffed,

and he PUFFED,

and he puffed,

and he HUFFED,

and he blew the little twig house all to pieces!

Away raced the two little pigs, straight to the
 third little pig’s house of bricks.

“Don’t worry,” said the third little pig to his two
 frightened little brothers. “You are safe here.” Soon
 they were all singing gaily.
