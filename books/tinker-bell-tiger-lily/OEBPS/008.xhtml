Just then, Iris flew by.

She spotted the tiger lily.

“Oh, Lily, you love every plant,

don’t you?” Iris said.

“Tiger lilies can be

nasty flowers.”

Lily blushed to her wingtips.

Iris held up her plant book.

She showed Lily the page

about tiger lilies.

“Be careful what you feed it,”

Iris warned Lily.

“No fairy food!”
