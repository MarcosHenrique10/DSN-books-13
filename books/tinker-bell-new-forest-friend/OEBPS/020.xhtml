Gruff’s fur is burned and his horns are broken. He is tired

from absorbing all the lightning and saving Pixie Hollow.

Gruff lowers himself next to Fawn and nudges her with his

nose. Fawn nuzzles him back.

“That’s my big furry monster!” she shouts.

The fairies cheer! Gruff has protected the fairies and saved

Pixie Hollow from the storm!
