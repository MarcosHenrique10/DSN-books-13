At dawn, the beast wakes Fawn with a nudge. Fawn

opens her eyes to see a giant tower of rocks curving into

the sky. It looks dangerous, twisted, and sharp. But it also

looks beautiful.

“Somebody’s a night owl,” Fawn says. “What is this?”

WHOOSH!

Suddenly, the beast grabs Fawn with his tail, places her

on top of his head, and begins to walk.

“Where to?” she asks. Then she adds, “Seriously,

what’s the tower for?”

The beast snarls.

“Well, you don’t have to be so…gruff about it,” she

complains. Then it hits her. “That’s it. That’s what I’ll

call you: Gruff.”
