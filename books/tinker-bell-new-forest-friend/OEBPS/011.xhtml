Fawn watches from afar as the beast moves toward

a big pile of rocks. He sorts through them, keeping

only the red rocks.

Fawn suspends one of the rocks from a tree.

When the beast stands high on his hind legs to get

the rock, she yanks the thorn from his front paw.

The beast howls,

licks his paw, and places

it on the ground gently.

He realizes Fawn has

helped him. He lets

out a grateful grunt.
