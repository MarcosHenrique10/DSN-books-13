Fawn decides to introduce Gruff to Tink and the fairies. “I’m sure

Gruff swings down from the tree, hanging upside down by his tail.

The fairies gasp, and Iridessa faints.

“What is that?” gulps Rosetta.

Fawn explains how she met Gruff and how other fairies in

Pixie Hollow think Gruff is a monster called the NeverBeast.

“I know he’s not what they say he is,” Fawn says. Fawn thinks he
