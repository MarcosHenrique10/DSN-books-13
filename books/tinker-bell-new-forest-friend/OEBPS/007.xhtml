She offers classes like Hopping 101. She greets three bunny

students: Calista, Nico, and Paige.

“Good morning, students!” Fawn says. “Let’s see that hopping.”

The bunnies hop up and down and side to side.

“Nice work, Nico! Perfection, Paige!” Fawn encourages.

Without warning, a loud moan booms through Pixie Hollow.

The bunnies quickly scamper away.
