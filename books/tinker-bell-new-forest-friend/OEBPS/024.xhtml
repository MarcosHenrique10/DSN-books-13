“Hey, big guy,” Fawn says. “I won’t see you again for a

while, but I know you’ll always be here when we need you.”

Gruff stomps in a few circles and settles into his bed. Fawn

kisses him on the nose and wishes him good night. They

smile at each other, and Fawn whispers, “I’m really going to

miss you. I love you, Gruff.”
