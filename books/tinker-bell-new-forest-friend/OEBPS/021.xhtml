The next morning, Gruff helps fix

up Pixie Hollow. He repairs things

that broke during the storm. He also

lifts heavy trees from the ground and

patches the roofs of fairy homes.

But soon he starts to get tired.

Fawn listens to his heartbeat and

knows that before long it will be

time for him to go back into

hibernation. She is sad, but she

knows that it is best for her furry

beast friend.
