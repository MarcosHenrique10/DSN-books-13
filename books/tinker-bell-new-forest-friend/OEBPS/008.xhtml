Fawn is curious, so she follows the noise

and winds up in a large opening. She spots a

patch of fur sticking out from beneath a rock.

Suddenly, she realizes she is standing in a

GIGANTIC paw print!

Carefully, Fawn follows the paw

prints and ends up outside a dark cave.

She flies in and finds a huge beast with

glowing green eyes, a thick snout, and

swirl-covered snow-white fur.
