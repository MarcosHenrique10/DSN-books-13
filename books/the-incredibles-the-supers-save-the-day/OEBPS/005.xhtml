In the city of Municiburg, everyone slept soundly. That’s because
 they knew Supers like Mr. Incredible would keep them safe. One night,
 a boy named Buddy tried to help Mr. Incredible stop a robbery.

Buddy didn’t have any powers, but he wanted to be Mr. Incredible’s
 sidekick.

But the Super didn’t want a sidekick. “I work alone,” he said. “Go
 home.” He made it very clear he didn’t want Buddy’s help again.
