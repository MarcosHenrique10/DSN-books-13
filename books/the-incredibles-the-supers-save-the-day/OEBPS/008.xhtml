So they started the Super Relocation Program, and gave all the
 supers new names and regular jobs. There was one condition: the
 Supers could not use their powers.

Mr. Incredible and Elastigirl became Bob and Helen Parr. They
 had three kids—Violet, Dash, and Jack-Jack. They tried to live a
 normal life, but sometimes things got crazy.

Violet could generate force fields and turn invisible, and Dash
 had super speed. Luckily, little Jack-Jack didn’t seem to have any
 powers yet.
