Meanwhile, back at home, Helen began to suspect that Bob
 was up to something.

She discovered that their old friend, Edna Mode, had made
 Bob a new Super suit. In fact, Edna had designed suits for the
 whole family!

“Each suit contains a homing device,” Edna told Helen.

Helen called a babysitter. Then she activated the device,
 called in a favor, and borrowed a jet.

Helen didn’t know Dash and Violet had stowed away until
 they were in the air.
