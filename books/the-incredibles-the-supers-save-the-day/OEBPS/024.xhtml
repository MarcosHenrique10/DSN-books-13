When they got there, they saw that Violet and Dash were

Suddenly, Syndrome showed up and used an immobi-ray
 on the whole family so that they couldn’t move. Then he flew
 off to the city of Metroville—where the Omnidroid was already

 the Omnidroid. Luckily, Violet was able to use a force field to
