Tink would get to the Winter Woods
 by hiding in one of the snowflake baskets!
 She snuck into a basket and hid. Then a
 snowy owl grabbed the basket and flew
 off! It was a very bumpy ride.

Soon, Tink arrived in the
 Winter Woods. There was just
 one problem—her book had
 fallen out of her satchel! A
 winter fairy spotted the book
 and picked it up. Tink secretly
 followed him.
