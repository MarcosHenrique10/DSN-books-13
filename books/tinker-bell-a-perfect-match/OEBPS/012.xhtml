First, Tinker Bell cut a warm
 winter coat out of a fuzzy leaf.

Next, she made a pair
 of furry snow boots.

Then she packed the rest of
 her things in a satchel.

Fully dressed for the cold,
 Tink tried to fly. But
 with her wings tucked
 inside her coat, she
 fell right over. Good
 thing she had a
 backup plan!
