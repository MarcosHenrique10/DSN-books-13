After a healing-talent fairy helped warm her wings, Tink
 headed to the library to do some research. She found what
 she was looking for—a book about wings. But a bookworm
 had eaten through some of the pages she needed!

Tink was told that she could learn more from the Keeper,
 who lived in the Winter Woods. There was only one thing
 for Tink to do. . . .
